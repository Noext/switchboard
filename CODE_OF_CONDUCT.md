# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, summarised below. The full text governs where this summary is silent.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone
is officially representing the project elsewhere. Report problems to the maintainers privately;
reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and may warn, temporarily or permanently ban any contributor whose behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
